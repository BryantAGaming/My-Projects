Mechanical Keyboard: Frequently Asked Questions

What would you like to know about my mechanical keyboards find the answers to your questions below!!!

What is a mechanical keyboard???

A mechanical keyboard is a keyboard that uses key switches rather than a membrane to register keystrokes.

What advantages do mechical keyboards???

Mechanical keyboards feel better to type on and enable some people to type faster.

Keyboard Feedback

Keyboard feedback can contribute to both typing enjoyment and speed.
