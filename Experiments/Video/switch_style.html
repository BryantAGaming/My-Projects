Which Key Switches Are Best for You?

Take the time to understand the different types of key switches available.

In a mechanical keyboard, each key has a serparte key switches located below the key cap, the button you press to issue the keystroke

You can choose from four main types of key switch makes a huge difference to your experience using the keyboard, so choose carefully.

Clicky Switches

Clicky switches give audible clicks each time you press down.

Tactile Switches

Tactile switches give a perceptible bumb but no click when you press them down.

Linear Switches

Linear switches give neither an audible click nor a perceptible bumb when pressed.
